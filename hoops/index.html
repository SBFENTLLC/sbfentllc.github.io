An outdoor 1v1 run. Break ankles, find your shot, leave your stamp.

HOW TO PLAY

Move with the left joystick. Hold SHOOT, then release when the meter reaches green. Get close to the rim for a dunk. CROSS gives a quick burst and briefly shakes your defender. On defense, get close and tap STEAL; near a shot, it becomes BLOCK. Stop moving for a steadier shot.

Inside the arc: 1 point. Outside: 2 points. First to 21 wins. A basket, steal, rebound or shot-clock violation starts a fresh possession at the top. Wins save on this device.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move, Space shoots, X crosses, E defends, Escape pauses. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen opens a separate app window.
