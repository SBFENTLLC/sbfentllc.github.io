    HOW TO PLAY

    Streetball controls: CROSS chains a crossover, behind-the-back and spin; hold backward for a step back. Change tricks quickly to build a style combo. Beat a nearby defender with a chain to break their ankles. Green releases, buckets, assists and defense fill STAMP. At 100%, drive inside and tap POWER to unleash your special for a 3-point finish.

    Crew Run: 3-on-3 against the computer. You control your player. PASS sends the ball to an open teammate; press PASS or SHOOT while a teammate has it to call the ball back. Cut to the rim and tap ALLEY OOP to call a lob, or lob to a cutting teammate. Teammates shoot if you leave them the ball. Defend the rival with the ball. In 1v1 and Practice, SELF LOB throws it off the glass: tap LOB again (or SHOOT) as the ball comes down to finish. Keyboard: F pass, L lob, R Stamp Breaker. Later story chapters are crew games.

    Crown Cup and Full Court: Pick from 16 squads with five players each. The Crown Cup plays three starters through four knockout rounds. Change your starters between games and win the final to earn a title. Full Court puts all five on the floor, with a basket at each end and two- and three-point scoring.

    My Player / Stamped Legacy: create your own player in the My Player panel, then Hit the Court. Read each scene and win the run to advance. Losses keep your current chapter. Earn XP, Style Rep and upgrade points. Crossovers, green releases, steals and dunks build your Style Rep. Dunk while ON FIRE for a Showtime finish and extra Style Rep. Finish all five chapters to become Crown Champion.

    Move with the left joystick. Hold SHOOT to gather, then release when the round meter above your player reaches green. Touch and hold the pause button to take a break. Drive inside and tap DUNK. A jump shot uses the meter at any distance. CROSS gives a quick burst and briefly shakes your defender. Hold SPRINT on offense for a speed boost; crossovers and sprinting use stamina that refills when you rest. On defense, tap STEAL near the ball handler or tap BLOCK as the shooter goes up. Stop moving for a steadier shot. Make three shots in a row to get ON FIRE and earn a small shooting boost. A miss clears your heat.

    Inside the arc: 1 point. Outside: 2 points. First to 21 wins. A basket, steal, rebound or shot-clock violation starts a fresh possession at the top. Earn 100 / 125 / 150 coins for a win by difficulty, 25 for finishing a loss, and bonus coins for buckets and steals. Practice has no defender or shot clock. Wins, coins, outfits and your best win streak save on this device.

    Keyboard: WASD / arrows move, Space shoots, X crosses, C dunks, E steals, B blocks, R activates Power, Shift sprints, Escape pauses. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen opens a separate app window.
