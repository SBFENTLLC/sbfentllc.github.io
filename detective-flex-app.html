DETECTIVE FLEX.
THE BIG OLE RIG CASES.

Step into four SBFENT locations. Walk the roads, look around, enter establishments, and search for the felt lizards. Pick a 60-second hunt or follow Detective Flex through the full story.

Story mode starts at Big Ole Rig. Find three outside and two inside each location to unlock the next case.

HOW TO PLAY DETECTIVE FLEX
  1. Start: Choose Regular or Story, then tap Start the search.
  2. Move: Hold the green MOVE joystick with your left thumb and slide it in the direction you want to walk. Lift your thumb to stop.
  3. Look around: Hold ↶ or ↷ with your right thumb to turn. You can move and turn together. Drag across the scene to look around, too.
  4. Catch lizards: Walk close to a green felt lizard to catch it automatically. Search for three outside and two inside each location.
  5. Find the entrance: Follow the green doorway marker on the map. When you are close enough, Find door changes to Enter. Tap it to go inside; tap Exit near the inside doorway to leave.
  6. Need a clue? Tap Scan to reveal lizard positions as green dots on the map for four seconds. Wait for the Scan countdown before using it again.

Regular: Catch as many lizards as you can in 60 seconds. Story: Find all five at each of four locations, then tap Next location—20 total, with no timer.

Tap Pause to take a break, then Resume search. Music is optional. On a computer: WASD / arrows move, Q / R turn, E enters or exits, Space scans, and Shift runs.

How to install
  • iPhone / iPad: Open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Leave Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.
  • Android: Open this page in Chrome. Tap Install Detective Flex when available, or open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer: Open this page in Chrome or Edge and use the install icon in the address bar or the browser’s app menu. In Safari on Mac, use File → Add to Dock when available.

Internet connection required. The same 3D game and controls are used in the browser and installed app.
