02 / THE ARTIST & FOUNDER

FLEX
A LOT.

SUMTER, SOUTH CAROLINA

RECORDING ARTIST / CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Built for the party.
Driven by legacy.

Flex A Lot makes music for the feeling: the chills when a record hits, the lift that changes your day, and the energy that turns a room into a party. Based in Sumter, South Carolina, the independent artist and founder of Stamped by Flex Ent, LLC brings a love of celebration and a family-first ambition to everything he creates.

His love for music began long before the studio. His mother loved poetry, and he remembers waking up to her singing as she went about her day. At eight, he made a mixtape on a two-sided cassette. In high school, he stayed up through the night mixing songs and burning stacks of CDs, rarely going anywhere without headphones. The joyful party records of Roscoe Dash and Travis Porter inspired him to start writing.

He moved to Sumter at 15, and the teenage years he enjoyed there helped shape his sound. South Carolina’s food, clubs, parks, beaches, and weather still keep him inspired. After a difficult transition following military service, he found renewed purpose in creating. He entered the studio at 27 and never looked back, determined to keep using the gift he had been given.

The name Flex A Lot reflects his love of flashy style and records made to move the club. Behind the shine is a bigger goal: provide for three generations of his family, then enjoy what he has built. Early on, he spent hours making beats on BandLab. When his first producer recognized the work and suggested a tag, they created “STAMPED BY FLEX” on the spot—a signature now heard across his music.

SBFENT carries that signature into a business with room for music, creativity, and entertainment. Its purpose is personal: give his children’s children—and the generations after them—a foundation they can build on. “MUSIC BUILDS LEGACIES” is the reason behind the work.

Fifty years from now, Flex wants a song of his to still brighten someone’s day and make them want to party. He hopes to be remembered for turning the party up, staying dripped out, taking care of his family, and being genuine. His idea of inspiration leaves room for everybody: if hearing his music pushes someone to step into a studio and make their own, he counts that as a win.

Stamped by Flex Ent, LLC

Stamped by Flex Ent, LLC grew from a studio signature. After seeing the hours Flex A Lot put into making beats on BandLab, his first producer encouraged him to create a tag. Together, they came up with “STAMPED BY FLEX”—the starting point for SBFENT.

Today, the vision reaches across music, media, creativity, and entertainment: build a business future generations can inherit, enjoy, and make their own. For Flex, “MUSIC BUILDS LEGACIES” begins with giving his family a foundation that lasts.
