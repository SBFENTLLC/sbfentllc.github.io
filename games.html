HOW TO PLAY DETECTIVE FLEX
  1. Start: Choose Standard, Quick challenge, Free Roam, or Story, then tap Start the search.
  2. Move: Tap an open spot on the scene to walk there, or hold the green MOVE joystick with your left thumb and slide it in the direction you want to walk. Lift your thumb to stop.
  3. Look around: Hold ↶ or ↷ with your right thumb to turn. You can move and turn together. Drag across the scene to look around, too.
  4. Catch lizards: Walk close to a green felt lizard to catch it automatically and earn 100 Flex Bucks. Story: find six outside and four inside each level. Regular: three outside and two inside. The Warehouse starting area has five inside witnesses per sweep.
  5. Investigate: Follow gold clue diamonds on the map in Story mode. Get close and tap Inspect or Verify clue. Nearby witnesses can be questioned; after a catch, tap Question to review their statement. On a computer, F opens the interaction. The timer pauses while you read. At the end of each Story case, compare the statements and records, then choose your deduction. Wrong answers give a clue without taking points. In regular play, find five witnesses to unlock an optional case review.
  6. Find the entrance: Follow the green doorway marker on the map. When you are close enough, Find door changes to Enter. Tap it to go inside; tap Exit near the inside doorway to leave.
  7. Need a clue? Tap Scan to reveal lizard positions as green dots on the map briefly. Story scans get shorter and take longer to recharge as levels increase. Wait for the Scan countdown before using it again.

Regular: Catch as many lizards as you can in 120 seconds. Choose Quick challenge for 60 seconds or Free Roam for no timer. Story: Clear 10 levels at each of four locations. Each level has 10 lizards and earns 1,000 Flex Bucks. Complete all 40 levels to find 400 lizards and earn 40,000 Flex Bucks. No timer.

Tap Pause to take a break, then Resume search. Story progress saves after every catch. Choose Story to continue your saved run. Restart story starts over while keeping your Flex Bucks. Music is optional. Your catch celebration plays the Stamped by Flex tag; tap Tag sound on to mute it. Rapid catches share one tag so sounds do not overlap. On a computer: WASD / arrows move, Q / R turn, E enters or exits, Space scans, and Shift runs.
