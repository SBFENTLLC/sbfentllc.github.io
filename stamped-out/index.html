All twelve fighting styles & counterplay

FLEX A LOT · Pressure boxing
Fast close-range punches. Confirm P → P → K after contact. Close-to-midrange hands; slower kicks.
Super: Stamped Out · launch and crown-stamp slam.

THE BOOTLEGGER · Trickster kickboxing
Long kicks can confirm into a punch. Slower movement; missed kicks leave a punish window.
Super: Counterfeit Combo · clone strikes and a flying kick.

FOXY · Evasive kickboxing
Fast retreat and K → K confirms. Light damage; the corner limits her retreat.
Super: Foxy Rush · zigzag kicks and a heel-drop finish.

CLARA · Aerial acrobatics
High jump and fast air movement. Her landing has a recovery window.
Super: Scarlet Spin · rising launch and a corkscrew kick finish.

GOLDIE · Power grappling
Heavy punches and 23-damage throws. Slow approach and short reach.
Combo: P → GRAB. Super: Gold Standard · lift and crushing slam.

ONYX · Counter striking
Tap BLOCK just before a punch or kick connects, then P for an 18-damage counter. A missed counter has long recovery.
Super: Onyx Reversal · evasive counter barrage.

DJ BLACKOUT · Sound-wave zoning
Ground K sends a traveling sound wave. Block it or jump over it, then punish his recovery.
Super: Bass Drop · rising bass and a final shockwave.

QUIET MONEY · Defensive boxing
Strong guard takes less chip; heavy P deals 16. Grabs break defense; missed punches leave an opening.
Super: Silent Investment · body work and a heavy straight.

NEON NOVA · Dash striking
Hold a direction + P to dash-punch. Fast approach, light hits, punishable miss.
Super: Neon Blitz.

BIG STAMP · Wrestling
26-damage throws, closer pressure after a slam. Slow approach; throws can be escaped.
Super: Crown Crusher.

VIOLET V · Capoeira
Ground K sweeps standing guard. Defend with DOWN + BLOCK or jump.
Super: Purple Reign.

REDLINE · Rushdown
Advancing P → P → P confirms. Blocking stops his chain and exposes recovery.
Super: Overdrive.

PHONE: turn sideways · left pad moves, ▲ jumps, ▼ crouches
KEYBOARD: arrows / WASD · J punch · K kick · L block · I grab · U special · Esc pause

COMBOS: tap the next attack as your hit lands. Whiffs and blocked attacks cannot chain.
GRAB: get close, hold left/right to choose the throw direction.
Tap GRAB quickly when caught to escape. Jumping avoids ground grabs.
