# Chapter-three art

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Project assets: `assets/iron-haul.webp` (eight isolated transparent poses; `iron-data.mjs` stores masks and hulls) and `assets/motoryard.webp` (Flex Motor Yard). WebP conversion preserves atlas transparency.

## Iron Haul prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: genuinely transparent RGBA production fighting-game sprite atlas, no backdrop or floor shadows. Style reference is only for chunky cel-illustrated arcade style, not character identity. Create original SBFENT boss IRON HAUL: dark-brown-skinned tall broad muscular Black man, short gray-tipped twists and close gray beard, purple industrial utility vest with lime crown patch over charcoal work shirt, reinforced charcoal/purple loose cargo trousers, lime seam accents, heavy gray high-top work boots, padded purple fingerless work gloves. Construction-yard heavyweight with streetwear personality, human not a robot. Exactly eight full-body sprites, four columns by two rows, all facing RIGHT, consistent identity, costume, scale. Top row ready stance, forward walking step, fully extended heavy straight punch, planted low wide bracing stance with both fists tucked. Bottom row crossed-arm guard, recoiling hurt, airborne bent-knee jump, leaning forward driving shoulder smash. Entire head, hands and both boots visible for every sprite, generous 35px clear gutters and no touching edges or overlap. Strong clean dark outlines, textured saturated cel shading. No labels, weapons, props, effects, glows or background. Landscape sprite atlas.

Style reference: existing Big Pressure atlas.

## Flex Motor Yard prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wide 16:9 production arena background for original SBFENT side-view 2D fighting game. FLEX MOTOR YARD at sunset: Southern custom auto garage and freight yard, purple-painted warehouse, a lifted customized pickup and a lowrider parked behind safety barriers, stacks of tires and motor parts on back walls, distant freight trailers, warm gold floodlights, lime and purple crown-themed garage murals, a few spectators behind fencing. Original street arcade atmosphere, textured hand-painted cel illustration. Straight level side-view camera with clear flat concrete fighting floor covering the bottom 38 percent. Keep all vehicles, columns, barriers, people, crates and props ABOVE the foreground floor horizon around 60 percent image height. Long clean fighting strip across entire width, nothing blocking fighters. Gold sunset sky and gritty purple/lime accents; background contrast should leave foreground fighter sprites readable. No fighters, HUD, lettering, watermark or brand-name vehicles.
