## The False King prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: genuine transparent RGBA production fighting-game sprite atlas, no backdrop, no floor shadow. Use reference only for chunky illustrated arcade style, not identity. Original SBFENT final boss THE FALSE KING: lean tall athletic dark-brown-skinned Black man, narrow beard, purple-tipped slicked-back short locs, gold-tinted angular sunglasses, a small chipped gold crown fitted directly onto his hair (not floating), black and royal-purple varsity jacket with gold trim and lime crown patches, black shirt, baggy purple/black cargo trousers, gold-purple high-top sneakers, gold fingerless gloves. Swaggering counterfeit royalty, no weapons or cape. Exactly EIGHT full-body separate sprites in FOUR columns and TWO rows, all facing RIGHT, consistent face, crown, outfit and proportions. Top row ready boxing stance, walking step, full extended straight punch, high kick. Bottom row crossed-arm guard, recoiling hurt, airborne knees tucked, broad open-arm royal power stance. All head/crown, fingers and both shoes fully inside every pose with 35px transparent gutters, no touching edges or overlapping sprites. Bold black outlines, saturated textured cel shading matching the reference game sprites. No words, labels, effects, glows, extra objects or background. Wide landscape sprite atlas.

Style reference: existing Iron Haul atlas.

## Crown Penthouse prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wide 16:9 production arena backdrop for original SBFENT side-view 2D fighting game. CROWN PENTHOUSE, a lavish purple and gold high-rise penthouse above a Southern US city at night. Huge floor-to-ceiling windows show a vivid city skyline, sculptural gold crown motif behind a raised lounge, purple velvet sofas and marble columns in background, lime accent lighting, expensive sound system, scattered counterfeit gold crown decorations. A final boss showdown setting, richly textured hand-painted arcade cel illustration. Straight level side-view camera, bottom 38 percent is a completely clear flat polished dark marble fighting floor with subtle gold/purple reflections. Keep every sofa, column, railing and decoration above the floor horizon near 60 percent image height. No props blocking the foreground fighting strip, no fighters or people, no HUD, no lettering, no watermarks. Foreground fighters will be large and colorful, so restrain floor contrast.
